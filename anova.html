ANOVA Analysis

Use this tool to perform analysis of variance (ANOVA) for agricultural and experimental data. Supported designs include one‑way and two‑way ANOVA, CRD, RCBD, augmented block design, simple ANOVA, Line × Tester, Diallel and mean comparison tests. Upload your Excel or CSV file and follow the on‑screen options to select the correct design, factors and traits.
