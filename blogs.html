Getting Started with Zulqarnain Statistical Tools

Posted on 2026-01-10

In this first blog post I introduce the main idea behind Zulqarnain Statistical Tools: to provide plant breeders and researchers with an easy web interface to run ANOVA, multivariate analysis and other statistical procedures using R in the background.

You only need to prepare your data in Excel or CSV format, upload it, choose the appropriate design or multivariate method, and then download publication‑ready tables and 300 dpi graphs.

Example: RCBD ANOVA for Yield Data

Posted on 2026-01-12

This example shows how to analyse a randomized complete block design (RCBD) with Zulqarnain Statistical Tools. We upload the yield data, specify block and treatment factors, and obtain ANOVA tables with F‑values, p‑values and mean comparison tests.

In future posts I will cover augmented block design, Line × Tester, Diallel and multivariate methods such as PCA, cluster analysis, correlation and path analysis.
