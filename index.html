Online statistical tools for ANOVA, multiple analysis, and related methods.

Using guidance

Zulqarnain Statistical Tools is designed to work like R software, but through a simple web interface. Follow these steps to analyse your experimental data and download publication‑ready outputs.

Step 1 – Prepare your data file

  • Organise your data in Excel (.xlsx) or CSV (.csv) format.
  • Use clear column names (for example: Treatment, Replication, Yield, Height).
  • Each row should represent one observation / experimental unit.
  • Remove empty rows and columns, and avoid merged cells or formulas.

Step 2 – Choose the appropriate tool

  • ANOVA: one‑way, two‑way, CRD, RCBD, augmented block design, simple ANOVA, Line × Tester, Diallel, mean comparison tests.
  • t‑test: comparison of two means (paired or independent samples).
  • Descriptive statistics: mean, standard deviation, standard error, minimum, maximum, coefficient of variation, etc.
  • Multivariate analysis: PCA (with biplot, scree plot and tables), cluster analysis (dendrogram and cluster tables), correlation matrix, heatmaps, path analysis with tree diagrams, and other graphs.

Step 3 – Upload your dataset

  • Click the menu item for the analysis you want (e.g. ANOVA Analysis or Multiple Analysis).
  • Use the Browse / Upload button to select your Excel or CSV file.
  • Select the correct sheet (for Excel files) and confirm the column names detected by the tool.

Step 4 – Set analysis options

  • Choose the response variable(s) and factor(s) (e.g. treatment, replication, year, location).
  • For ANOVA, select the experimental design (CRD, RCBD, augmented, Line × Tester, Diallel, etc.).
  • For PCA, cluster analysis, correlation or path analysis, choose the traits to include in the multivariate model.
  • Adjust optional settings such as significance level (α), type of mean comparison test, distance measure for clustering, etc.

Step 5 – Run the analysis

  • Click the Run or Analyse button for the chosen method.
  • The server will process your data using R, and results will appear on the screen as:
    • ANOVA tables, error terms, F‑values and p‑values,
    • t‑test or mean comparison tables,
    • PCA tables, eigenvalues, loadings, biplots and scree plots,
    • Cluster tables and dendrogram plots,
    • Correlation matrices, heatmaps and path diagrams.

Step 6 – Download tables and graphs

  • Use the Download table buttons to save results as Excel files for further editing or reporting.
  • Use the Download plot buttons to save figures as 300 dpi PNG images suitable for theses, presentations or journal publications.

Step 7 – Interpret and report your results

  • Interpret F‑values, p‑values, mean comparisons and multivariate patterns just as you would in R.
  • Always cross‑check the assumptions of the methods (normality, homogeneity of variance, independence, etc.).
  • When using these results in publications, theses or reports, please give proper credit to Zulqarnain Statistical Tools (see Terms and Conditions below).
